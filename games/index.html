Why these Wear OS games are different

Made for tiny sessions

Quick loops, clear state, and simple controls so the game works while commuting, waiting, or taking a short break.

Native watch controls

Rotary bezel or crown support is treated as a first-class input, not a novelty added after the fact.

Battery-aware design

Dark OLED presentation, careful frame targets, and lightweight UI keep the experience practical on real watches.

Built for modern Wear OS watches

The current collection targets Wear OS 3+ and Android smartwatches, with testing focused on the constraints that matter on wrist hardware: circular safe zones, heat, touch size, short attention windows, and readable motion.

Start with Cyberpunk 3D if you want a visual smartwatch benchmark, or Feed Me, Loser! if you want a funny virtual pet game made for your wrist.
