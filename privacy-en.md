    <h1>Privacy Policy</h1>
    <p class="updated">Last updated: May 31, 2026</p>

    <section>
        <h2>1. Information We Collect</h2>
        <p>
            We do not collect, store, or transmit any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your name, email address, physical address, or precise location.
        </p>
        <p>
            To optimize performance and ensure stability on Wear OS smartwatches, our applications collect anonymous, non-personal technical data:
        </p>
        <ul>
            <li><strong>Device & Hardware Specifications:</strong> Device model, CPU core count, RAM size, GPU name, operating system version, and screen resolution.</li>
            <li><strong>Performance Metrics:</strong> Frame rate (FPS), frame stutters, memory consumption, and battery drainage rate during gameplay or benchmarking.</li>
            <li><strong>App Diagnostics:</strong> Anonymous crash reports and error logs (stack traces) to help us identify and fix software bugs.</li>
            <li><strong>Anonymized Gameplay Events:</strong> Basic in-game actions (e.g., starting a session, game scores, or game-over events) to analyze general user interaction.</li>
        </ul>

        <h2>2. Third-Party Services & Data Processors</h2>
        <p>
            Our applications utilize third-party SDKs provided by Unity Technologies to collect diagnostics and performance telemetry:
        </p>
        <ul>
            <li><strong>Unity Analytics:</strong> Generates a random, temporary installation ID to differentiate active sessions and gather hardware statistics.</li>
            <li><strong>Unity Cloud Diagnostics:</strong> Captures and transmits exception logs and crash details.</li>
        </ul>
        <p>
            These services process data in accordance with Unity’s privacy policies. You can learn more about how Unity manages data by visiting the 
            <a href="https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Unity Privacy Policy</a>.
        </p>
        <p>
            Our apps are distributed via Google Play, which also collects certain automated telemetry in accordance with the 
            <a href="https://policies.google.com/privacy" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Google Privacy Policy</a>.
        </p>

        <h2>3. Data Deletion Requests & Contact</h2>
        <p>
            If you wish to request the deletion of your anonymous performance or diagnostics telemetry data, or if you have any other questions, contact us at
            <a href="mailto:wristandpocket.studio@gmail.com">wristandpocket.studio@gmail.com</a>.
        </p>
    </section>
