### Welcome to the Official Developer Log of Wrist & Pocket Studio!

Here we are—a tiny indie studio focused on making fun, optimized, and genuinely exciting games for smartwatches. Our goal is simple and ambitious: we want to make watch gaming great again!

Let's be honest: everyone is tired of the trash that usually gets released on Wear OS. Have you seen those "masterpieces"—bundles of 3, 5, or sometimes even 10 primitive games in a single app? Good lord, what a cringe fest in the Play Store...

That's why, instead of these trash titles, we create real **GAMES** that are actually fun to play. Something to get hooked on while commuting, to show to your friends or family, and just to have a good time.

### What W&P Studio is Working On:

Right now, work is in full swing on two cool projects:

- **Cyberpunk 3D** — the studio's debut. It's a technological 3D benchmark where you can enjoy beautiful graphics while finding out the real performance power of your smartwatch. Two birds with one stone, holy moly!
- **Feed Me, Loser!** — a funny and cheeky virtual pet living right on your wrist. Feed it, keep it entertained, but watch out—not everything in this game is as simple as it seems. P.S. It will be explosive!

Thank you for being with us. Subscribe and follow for updates—the best is yet to come!
